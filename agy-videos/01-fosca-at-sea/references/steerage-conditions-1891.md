# Reference: Steerage Conditions & Visual Records (1889–1891)

This document compiles the contemporary visual and descriptive records used to reconstruct the physical atmosphere of transatlantic steerage for the video production.

---

## 1. Steerage Interior (1891)

- **Source ID**: `steerage-interior-1891`
- **Citation**: John H. Gould, *“Ocean Passenger Travel”*, *Scribner’s Magazine* (1891); preserved by GG Archives.
- **Associated Asset**: [`../images/steerage-interior-1891.webp`](file:///Users/leo/dev/history/agy-videos/01-fosca-at-sea/images/steerage-interior-1891.webp)
- **Documentary Reality**:
  - The illustration depicts the cramped physical arrangement of transatlantic steerage in 1891: multi-tiered wooden bunks directly against the ship's frames, narrow benches, tin dinner pails, bundles hanging from ceiling hooks, and families living in close quarters.
  - Natural light was minimal, filtered through small deck prisms or open companionway hatches during calm seas, while oil lanterns provided dim, flickering illumination at night.

## 2. Emigrant Family on Deck (1889)

- **Source ID**: `emigrant-family-on-deck-ferraguti`
- **Citation**: Arnaldo Ferraguti, illustration for Edmondo De Amicis, *Sull’Oceano* (Milan: Fratelli Treves, 1889).
- **Associated Asset**: [`../images/emigranti_ponte_ferraguti.jpg`](file:///Users/leo/dev/history/agy-videos/01-fosca-at-sea/images/emigranti_ponte_ferraguti.jpg)
- **Visual Reference Value**:
  - Shows an authentic contemporary Italian peasant family huddled on the open wooden deck among ropes, tarpaulins, and wicker trunks.
  - Mother wrapped in a dark woolen shawl and headscarf, cradling an infant close to her body.
  - Father and older children dressed in rough working woolens, sitting together against the ship's bulwarks to escape the foul air of the lower holds.

## Important Methodological Audit Note

- **Direct Primary Evidence**:
  - The photograph of the steamship *Colombo* ([`colombo-drydock-1901.jpg`](file:///Users/leo/dev/history/agy-videos/01-fosca-at-sea/images/colombo-drydock-1901.jpg)) and the Hospedaria ledger ([`panzonato-hospedaria-1891-page.jpg`](file:///Users/leo/dev/history/agy-videos/01-fosca-at-sea/images/panzonato-hospedaria-1891-page.jpg)) are **direct records** of the vessel and the Panzonato family.
- **Contextual Sensory Evidence**:
  - The Ferraguti illustration, Scribner's steerage engraving, and Gaensly photographs are **contemporary comparative records** representing the authentic physical world of the era, rather than depictions of the Panzonato family itself.
  - In our video production, these images act as strict visual ground truth for lighting, costume textures, ship architecture, and framing.
